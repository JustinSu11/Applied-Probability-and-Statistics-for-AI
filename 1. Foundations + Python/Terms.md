Descriptive:
	Summarize the observed data
		Cases, Variables, Values
		Distributions: Center, Variability, Shape
		Association and Comparison

Inferential:
	Generalize beyond the observed data
		Population, Sample
		Parameter Statistic
	Inferential Statistics is to infer upon a population from the data of a sample

Cases - Who or what is observed (subject)
	One transaction 
	One patient
	One prompt

Variables - What is measured (categories)
	Latency
	Outcome
	Model family

Values - The recorded result
	242 ms
	Approved
	Transformer

Types of variables:
	Quantitative - numbered value:
		Discrete - 
		Continuous - 
	Categorical
		Ordinal - Hierarchy to categories
		Nominal - No hierarchy 

Bar chart - Compare categories
Histogram - Inspect on quantitative data

Mean (**Parametric**)- Average value 
Median (**Non-Parametric**)- Middle value (if middle is between two values, get the average of the two) 
Mode - Most frequent value

Variability tells us how much the observation differ
	Standard Deviation (SD) - Typical distance from the mean
